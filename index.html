CanBudget Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

CanBudget is a budgeting app for Canadians. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version: everything you enter stays on your phone, and we never receive it.

What CanBudget stores

Whatever you type into the app: your pay schedule and amounts, bills, debts, savings and registered accounts (TFSA, FHSA, RRSP, RESP and others), goals, insurance and protection details, and your payment history. This information is saved only in the app's storage on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. CanBudget has no accounts, no sign-in, no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to your bank. It does not send your data to us or to anyone else, and we cannot see it.

Who we share data with

No one. We have no access to your data, so there is nothing to share, sell or disclose to third parties.

Device features the app uses

  • Notifications. If you turn on reminders, the app schedules bill and debt reminders on your device. They are created and shown locally.
  • Device lock. If you turn on app lock, the app asks your phone's own screen lock, fingerprint or face unlock to confirm it is you. The app never sees or stores your biometric data. Your phone handles it.

Backups

CanBudget does not back up to any cloud. Automatic Android cloud backup is turned off for the app. You can export a backup file from Settings and save it wherever you choose. If you share that file, for example by email, that is your decision, and the file's contents are then handled by the service you chose. You can restore from that file later.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything from Settings using "Delete all data". Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold no copy, deleting it on your phone removes it completely. A backup file you exported yourself is not deleted automatically, so remove it if you no longer want it.

Children

CanBudget is meant for adults and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
